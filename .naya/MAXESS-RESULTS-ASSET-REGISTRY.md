- Every image must have a narrative job.
- Preserve aspect ratio and crop intentionally.
- Keep portraits recognizable at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Use descriptive alt text where the image carries meaning.
- Do not hard-code temporary replacement assets when an approved asset exists.
