# MAXESS Results Experience Flow — V17 Locked Direction

Status: LOCKED DESIGN DIRECTION
Branch: `maxess-results-v16-updated-edited`
Authoritative Results foundation: `MAXESS-RESULTS-10-GROOVE.html`
Runtime boundary: `window.MAXESS_RESULT`

## Purpose

This document locks the approved human experience and section order for the next MAXESS Results refinement.

The goal is not to add more features. The goal is to create a coherent personal AI mastery report that moves the human through:

DATA → INSIGHT → UNDERSTANDING → ACTION → CAPABILITY.

## Locked Experience Order

### 0. Naya Orientation Banner — very top

A compact Naya introduction is pinned at the very top of the page, above the Orb.

Core framing:

“This isn't a judgment. It's a map.”

Purpose:
- Orient the person before presenting the score.
- Make the report feel personal rather than clinical.
- Introduce Naya as the intelligence guiding the interpretation.
- Keep this compact so it does not compete with the Orb hero.
- Ensure text contrast and readability are excellent.

### 1. YOUR AI SCORE — Hero

The approved Orb is the visual crown jewel and primary feature presentation.

Requirements:
- The Orb is the first major visual focus after the Naya orientation banner.
- Display `YOUR AI SCORE` once only.
- Display one authoritative runtime score inside the Orb.
- The score must derive from `window.MAXESS_RESULT`.
- No hard-coded production score.
- No duplicate score presentation.
- Preserve and refine the existing orbiting-ball motion that has already proven visually strong.
- Optimize scale, spacing, legibility, accessibility, responsiveness, and reduced-motion behavior without unnecessarily rebuilding the working Orb architecture.

### 2. Five AI Capability Mini-Orbs

Immediately after the hero, present the five assessment dimensions as a visual snapshot.

Use five mini-Orbs with the same visual language as the primary Orb.

The five values must be runtime-derived from `window.MAXESS_RESULT`.

Desktop goal:
- Five mini-Orbs arranged cleanly across the available content width.
- Each displays its dimension name and score.

Mobile goal:
- Responsive layout with no horizontal overflow.
- Preserve visual hierarchy and readability.

Purpose:
- Show what contributes to the overall score.
- Make the dimensions feel like capabilities rather than dashboard cards.

### 3. LISTEN TO NAYA

The primary interpretation CTA follows the score snapshot.

Use:

LISTEN TO NAYA

Do not use “See Your Results” as the primary CTA here because the person is already viewing the results.

Narrative logic:

SCORE → SNAPSHOT → NAYA

### 4. YOUR PATTERN

Reveal the meaningful relationship between the person's dimension scores.

The section should answer:

“What pattern do these scores reveal about me?”

It must move beyond repeating numbers.

### 5. HERE'S WHAT IT MEANS

Interpret the pattern in plain, human language.

The report should translate data into understanding.

Avoid generic assessment language. The interpretation should feel specific to the individual's result data.

### 6. YOUR STRENGTH

Identify the person's meaningful existing advantage.

Do not merely repeat the highest numerical score. Explain what the strength enables the person to do with AI.

### 7. YOUR LEVER

Identify the highest-leverage development opportunity.

Frame an opportunity as leverage, not as a deficiency or judgment.

The user should understand why improving this capability matters and how it could amplify existing strengths.

### 8. YOUR ACTION

Provide the clearest practical next action.

Keep the action focused. Avoid overwhelming the user with a long task list.

Narrative progression:

PATTERN → MEANING → STRENGTH → LEVER → ACTION

### 9. VIDEO / YOUR MOVE / FREE TRIAL

The conversion moment follows the interpretation/action sequence.

The “Your Move” section should be simplified aggressively.

Keep:

YOUR MOVE

WATCH THE VIDEO

START YOUR FREE TRIAL

The video and free-trial action should feel like one coherent next-step decision point.

Remove unnecessary boxes, repeated explanatory copy, and decorative conversion clutter.

### 10. INCLUDES EVERYTHING — 18 Naya Masters

The 18 Naya Masters remain toward the bottom of the report, after the video and free-trial decision point.

The “Includes Everything” message introduces the complete capability library.

The 18 Masters should be presented as personalized AI capability profiles, not as a generic catalogue.

Each Master should be capable of communicating, where supported by the Result Contract:
- Master identity/profile
- What the capability does
- Relevant user score/status or relationship
- Why it matters to this person
- Why Naya recommends or prioritizes it
- Practice/development direction

The 18 Masters are part of the deeper product/capability layer and must not interrupt the primary results narrative above the conversion moment.

### 11. DON'T LEARN AI. LEARN WHAT AI CAN DO FOR YOU.

This section comes after the 18 Naya Masters.

The Playground should be the experiential invitation to discover AI capability in practice.

Core message:

DON'T LEARN AI.
LEARN WHAT AI CAN DO FOR YOU.

This is a transition from understanding the report to experiencing capability.

### 12. Naya + Technology — Final Ending

The existing approved Naya/technology ending remains at the absolute bottom.

Core closing philosophy:

“Technology should amplify the human.”

Do not move this back to the top.
Do not use it as the opening headline.
Do not add unnecessary conversion elements to the ending.

It should function as the final human/philosophical thought after the complete experience.

## Editorial Rules

For every existing Results section, use only these decisions:

- KEEP — already earns its existence.
- MOVE — valuable but belongs elsewhere in the locked sequence.
- REWRITE — strong concept, weak communication.
- MERGE — duplicates another section.
- REMOVE — does not earn its space.

Do not solve weak sections by automatically adding more features or more copy.

The preferred direction is editorial compression, stronger hierarchy, clearer narrative, and deeper personalization.

## Why This Flow Is Locked

The sequence follows the natural human journey:

1. Naya establishes how to interpret the report.
2. The Orb creates an immediate emotional understanding of the score.
3. The five mini-Orbs provide a fast capability snapshot.
4. Naya becomes the guide.
5. The report turns numbers into pattern and meaning.
6. Meaning becomes strength and leverage.
7. Leverage becomes an actionable next move.
8. The video and free trial provide the immediate product action.
9. The 18 Masters reveal the depth of what is included.
10. The Playground turns learning into experience.
11. The Naya/technology message closes the experience with the human-centered philosophy.

The report should therefore feel like a personal journey, not a decorated dashboard.

AAA is not a label. AAA is what survives inspection.
