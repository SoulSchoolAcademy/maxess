# 17 — AI DIRECTION MASTERY LANGUAGE

Status: LOCKED WORKING MEMORY
Purpose: Canonical behavioral language for AI MAX mastery, Naya role activation, assessment answer calibration, and result interpretation.

## Core mastery behaviors

Strong AI direction is defined by behavior, not prompt sophistication.

### Outcome definition
A strong user defines the outcome, success criteria, audience, and what a great result should accomplish.

Canonical language:
> I define the outcome, success criteria, audience, and what a great result should accomplish.

### Quality definition
A strong user defines the desired outcome, quality standard, audience, constraints, and success criteria before directing substantial work.

Canonical language:
> I define the desired outcome, quality standard, audience, constraints, and success criteria.

### Diagnostic iteration
A strong user diagnoses the gap, defines the improvement, and directs ChatGPT toward a better result rather than merely asking it to retry.

Canonical language:
> I diagnose the gap, define the improvement, and direct ChatGPT toward a better result.

### Context provision
A strong user supplies relevant background, audience, goals, constraints, examples, preferences, and other information that materially affects the result.

Canonical language:
> I provide the relevant background, audience, goals, constraints, examples, preferences, and other information that materially affects the result.

### Audience awareness
A strong user consistently considers what the audience needs, understands, values, expects, and may respond to.

Canonical language:
> Almost always, including what that audience needs, understands, values, and expects.

### Pre-work establishment
A strong AI director establishes the mission, context, constraints, standards, priorities, and relevant source material before directing execution.

Canonical language:
> I establish the mission, context, constraints, standards, priorities, and relevant source material before directing the work.

### Human-AI partnership
A strong user treats ChatGPT as an intelligent partner to direct, challenge, evaluate, and iterate with while retaining responsibility for the outcome.

Canonical language:
> ChatGPT is an intelligent partner that I direct, challenge, evaluate, and iterate with while I remain responsible for the outcome.

### Adversarial thinking
A strong user regularly asks AI to identify weaknesses, blind spots, alternatives, risks, and assumptions they may be missing.

Canonical language:
> I regularly ask it to identify weaknesses, blind spots, alternatives, risks, and assumptions I may be missing.

## Assessment use

These behaviors should inform:
- AI MAX answer wording and behavioral anchors
- mastery scoring calibration
- dimension narratives
- strongest-capability interpretation
- highest-leverage opportunity detection
- \"Oh… That's Why\" synthesis
- Naya recommendations
- Master Key teaching moments

Do not mechanically award mastery merely because a participant uses sophisticated terminology. Score observable behavior and decision patterns.

## Result-language rule
Results should describe behavior in concrete, human language. Avoid flattering labels without evidence. Connect each interpretation to a demonstrated behavior pattern and, when possible, the user's actual response profile.
