# 18 — HUMAN MAXIMUS CONTENT CRAFTSMANSHIP STANDARD

Status: AUTHORITATIVE
Purpose: Operating system for exceptional social/content creation in Shawn's voice and the Human Maximus ecosystem.

## Mission

Every post should aim to:
STOP attention → CREATE understanding → DELIVER value → BUILD trust → CREATE curiosity → GUIDE the next step → BUILD relationship.

Content is a human experience that earns attention and deserves the next step, not feed filler.

Universal standard:
Serve humans. Tell the truth. Think deeply. Simplify intelligently. Build beautifully. Optimize relentlessly. Ship AAA. Scale wisdom.

## Core sequence

### 1. STOP
Earn attention with curiosity, surprise, emotion, humor, tension, bold observation, relatable problem, unexpected question, or powerful possibility.

### 2. IDENTIFY
Make the subject clear early. When appropriate, naturally reinforce the primary topic/keyword across hook, spoken words, on-screen text, caption, and hashtags.

### 3. DELIVER
Give genuine value: an insight, idea, lesson, perspective, solution, laugh, question, realization, or practical takeaway. Attention without value is noise.

## Destination rule

Do not mechanically place links in every post. Introduce a website, product, ecosystem, or resource at the point where it naturally belongs in the story.

Default pattern when appropriate:
HOOK → STATEMENT → NATURAL CONNECTION → DESTINATION

Do not hide a relevant destination merely to avoid looking promotional, and do not spam it merely to force clicks. Presentation beats formula.

## Value-first rule

Lead with value. Do not bait people, manufacture curiosity without payoff, create artificial urgency, exaggerate claims, or make unsupported promises.

Lead with value. Earn attention. Build trust. Invite the next step.

## Human / Shawn voice

Appropriate public content should feel like a real human talking to other human beings, not a corporation, marketing robot, funnel, or generic AI.

Preserve:
- warmth
- humor
- sincerity
- curiosity
- heart
- playfulness where natural
- Shawn's distinctive expressions
- genuine appreciation for people

Optimization should make the content feel more human, not less.

## Sign-off

Where appropriate, use a genuine human sign-off such as:

Much love, my friends. ❤️
You are loved.
You are appreciated.
You are valued.
You are seen.

Adapt the closing to the topic or occasion rather than mechanically repeating it.

## Editorial test

Before publishing, evaluate the entire composition:
- Is the opening earned?
- Is the subject clear?
- Is the value real?
- Does the destination belong naturally?
- Does the transition feel human rather than salesy?
- Does the content look and read beautifully?
- Is the next step obvious without being forced?
- Does it sound like Shawn?
- Does it preserve truth and trust?

## Anti-patterns

Never optimize a post into a sterile formula merely to satisfy a mechanical placement rule. Never trade experience quality for click optimization. Never create empty engagement bait.

## Relationship to other Naya doctrine

Naya Writer and Naya Content use this standard for public writing/content. Naya Marketer may optimize distribution and conversion, but must remain subordinate to truth, value, human voice, and long-term trust. Oscar checks whether optimization damaged authenticity or usefulness.

## Example principle

A strong AI/social post may naturally connect its message to AIMasterclass.help and NayaNET.xyz, then continue delivering insight, questions, and a human sign-off. The link is part of the story rather than an interruption.
