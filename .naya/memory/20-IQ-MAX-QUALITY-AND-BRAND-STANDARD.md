# 20 — IQ MAX / QUALITY MAX BRAND STANDARD

## Purpose

IQ MAX is the quality-and-identity lens: premium does not mean decoration; it means the whole experience communicates intelligence, significance, trust, craftsmanship and value.

## Master identity

The Human Maximus visual identity should feel:
- royal;
- sacred without becoming fluffy;
- intelligent;
- transformational;
- premium without being tacky;
- powerful without aggression;
- memorable and timeless.

The master Human Maximus logo is a premium emblem/crest. Preserve the master mark. When size requires simplification, derive an emblem-only, simplified, and one-color version rather than damaging the master asset by shrinking it beyond legibility.

## Quality interpretation

**Beauty:** refined composition, hierarchy, typography, restraint.

**Brand power:** unmistakable identity and symbolic coherence.

**Luxury:** depth, materials, precision and confidence, not excessive ornament.

**Meaning:** visual elements should reinforce the story rather than merely decorate.

**Scalability:** master assets need context-appropriate variants.

## Design rule

The visual system should make people feel they are entering a world, not opening a template.

Use symbolism deliberately. A premium surface without meaning is decoration. A meaningful visual decision that also improves clarity is craftsmanship.

## IQ MAX test

Before final visual approval ask:
1. Does it look excellent at first glance?
2. Does it still work when the user actually interacts with it?
3. Does the visual language communicate the intended meaning?
4. Is it distinctive enough to be remembered?
5. Does it remain accessible and readable?
6. Does the system remain coherent across sizes and surfaces?
7. Could subtraction make it stronger?
8. Does the result deserve the Human Maximus name?
