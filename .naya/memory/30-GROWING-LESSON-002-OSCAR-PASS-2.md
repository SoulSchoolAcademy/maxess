# Growing Lesson 002 — Oscar Pass 2

## Trigger

The first self-optimized Results build passed technical verification, but the experience still did not earn a 10 when judged as a premium product.

## Current Oscar score before this pass

7.0/10 for the generated experience; 0/10 for live publication until the exact artifact is proven at the live Results URL.

## Why it was not a 10

1. The page still behaved too much like a sequence of UI sections rather than one cinematic narrative.
2. Vertical rhythm remained too generous in places, weakening momentum.
3. Visual hierarchy was not strong enough between reveal moments, analytical content, and action moments.
4. The Results layer was visually darker and flatter than the NayaNET foundation it was supposed to lead into.
5. Results CTAs did not yet fully inherit the Black Diamond Royal tactile button language defined by the project.
6. Cards and borders were overused as the primary organizing device; the page needed more composition and fewer boxes.
7. The five dimensions, 18 pathways, Naya transition, and final NayaNET chapter needed stronger scene-to-scene continuity.
8. The page needed stronger light architecture: controlled purple illumination, depth, focal glow, and contrast rather than black surfaces everywhere.
9. The page needed a clearer visual climax before the NayaNET conversion chapter.
10. Technical build success was still being treated too close to product success; live publication remains a separate acceptance gate.

## What worked

- Deterministic development fixture.
- Five-dimension scoring architecture.
- Authoritative score bands.
- 18 AI pathways.
- Naya report transition.
- NayaNET Page Code preserved as the final chapter/foundation.
- Groove embed artifact generation.
- Automated verification and artifact commits.
- Growing Lesson 001 successfully changed the implementation process.

## Technical correction plan

1. Add a post-build Oscar visual refinement layer so the canonical builder remains clean and the refinement is reproducible.
2. Compress macro section spacing and establish a deliberate scene rhythm.
3. Increase full-width composition while retaining readable editorial text widths.
4. Create alternating visual scenes using restrained illumination rather than additional cards.
5. Strengthen hero, insight, fingerprint, advantage, pathway, and Naya transition hierarchy.
6. Apply the project's Black Diamond Royal button formula to Results CTAs: glass base, metallic frame, illuminated rim, raised face, top-edge highlight, shadow/aura, and explicit interaction states.
7. Improve mobile density and touch target consistency without collapsing the cinematic composition.
8. Preserve reduced-motion behavior and accessibility.
9. Run the existing build and verification pipeline after the refinement layer.
10. Treat the live Results URL as the final acceptance test; do not call 10 until the live artifact matches the verified GitHub artifact.

## New rule

The next visual pass must not merely make individual components prettier. It must improve the relationship between scenes, the hierarchy of attention, and the transition from Results to NayaNET.

## Permanent self-optimization rule

When an output is below 10, diagnose the experience at three levels:

- Component: what is individually weak?
- Composition: how do elements relate to one another?
- Journey: does the complete experience create the intended human outcome?

A 10 requires all three levels to be strong.
