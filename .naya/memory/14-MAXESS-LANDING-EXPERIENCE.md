# 14 — MAXESS LANDING EXPERIENCE

Status: STRATEGIC PRODUCT MEMORY
Version: 1.0

## Landing-page job
The landing page has one primary job: make the visitor think, “That is exactly my problem. I want to know where I stand.” Then move them into the free assessment with minimal friction.

## Core opening
Problem: frustration with ChatGPT/AI not producing the quality/results the user wants.
Promise: free ~3-minute AI Mastery Assessment to discover strengths, where results are being left on the table, and what to improve.
Core friction reducer: 15 questions • ~3 minutes • immediate results • immediate feedback • free.
Do not interrupt the primary conversion with an email gate. Intended primary journey: Landing → Assessment → Results → Free Trial.

## Suggested psychological sequence
FRUSTRATION → PROMISE → 3 CLEAR BENEFITS → PERSONAL RELEVANCE (“What are you using AI for?”) → CREDIBILITY → PROOF / REAL-WORLD APPLICATION → WHO IS SHAWN? → BROADER ECOSYSTEM / AUTHORITY → 15 QUESTIONS / 3 MINUTES / FREE / IMMEDIATE RESULTS.

## Three benefits
The three benefits must be simple and human-readable, not technical jargon. They should communicate immediate result, deeper understanding, and personalized insight / next action.

## AI use-case selector
An optional interactive section may let users identify what they use AI for. Purpose: personal relevance and later connection to the appropriate one of the 18 AI Mastery Areas. It must not interrupt or weaken the primary psychological flow.

## Credibility / proof
Potential framing: this is a method developed and applied through real-world work, not merely a generic quiz. Proof may include ProMax Player, MAXESS itself, and other real products/systems created using the methodology. Any claim such as “proven,” “9.5+,” or performance improvement must be supported by real evidence before public use; never present unsupported performance guarantees as facts.

## Shawn / about
Use a concise credibility section answering: Who is Shawn? What does he do? Why did he create this? Why should the visitor trust him? Avoid turning the landing page into a full biography.

## Ecosystem links
Broader ecosystem properties can reinforce authority and continuity, but links should not overwhelm or distract from the assessment CTA.

## Methodology story
Where useful, explain that the methodology was researched, tested and applied to real work. Use this to bridge claims to evidence.

## Final CTA
Repeat the low-friction promise: 15 questions, ~3 minutes, immediate results, immediate intelligent feedback, free.

## Section-earning law
Every landing-page section must increase understanding, trust, desire or action. If it does not, remove it.
