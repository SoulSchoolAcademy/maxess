# 11 — AI MASTER KEY + AI MAX ASSESSMENT

Status: AUTHORITATIVE WORKING MEMORY

## 1. AI MAX ASSESSMENT

Flagship MAXESS assessment: **AI MAX / AI Mastery Assessment**.

Purpose:
- Measure how effectively a person currently works with AI.
- Produce useful self-understanding, not merely a score.
- Give small useful insight after each question.
- End with an honest score, profile, strongest capability, opportunity, and practical next step.
- Use the result to connect naturally to Naya and the wider Human Maximus/NayaNET ecosystem.

Public positioning:
- "What's Your ChatGPT Score?"
- "Discover how well you're REALLY using AI."
- 15 simple questions.
- About 3 minutes.
- No trick questions; no bad answers.
- Ask participants to answer based on what they actually do today, not what they think they should do.

Core dimensions and question groups:
1. Direction — Q1–Q3
2. Context — Q4–Q6
3. Collaboration — Q7–Q9
4. Evaluation — Q10–Q12
5. Iteration — Q13–Q15

Each dimension contributes 20% of the overall result.

Current supplied answer-value model uses five behavioral levels (0–4) with deliberately non-linear answer ordering. Before production, normalize each question response to a standardized contribution so no question accidentally carries extra weight because multiple answers appear strong.

The key psychological intent is to measure actual behavior, not self-image. Questions should help the participant notice useful truths about how they currently use AI.

## 2. RESULTS PHILOSOPHY

The result must answer:
- What did I score?
- What does that actually mean?
- What am I naturally good at?
- Where is my biggest opportunity?
- Why did I get this result?
- What does this say about how I work with AI?
- What should I learn next?
- What should I do next?

The experience should create:
**curiosity → recognition → understanding → possibility → action**.

Core Result message:
"Oh… that's why."

A score is a starting point, not a judgment.

## 3. MASTER KEY

The Human Maximus Master Key teaches people how to work with AI. It is not a prompt library. It is a practical mental operating system.

Core seven-word loop:
**KNOW → TELL → ASK → CREATE → SCORE → IMPROVE → FREEZE**

Expanded workflow:
1. Decide what you are trying to create/accomplish.
2. Choose the appropriate specialized area.
3. Load the Master Key and relevant specialized guide.
4. Give Naya the goal in natural language.
5. Ask Naya to ask only the questions she actually needs.
6. Let Naya answer what she reasonably can from known context.
7. Create the first version.
8. Score the result.
9. Ask: **"Why is this not a 10?"**
10. Preserve what works; diagnose and fix weaknesses.
11. Score again.
12. Check for regression.
13. Freeze the approved version.

Core philosophy:
- Humans provide intention, direction, judgment, and approval.
- AI performs heavy lifting it can reasonably perform.
- Specialized guides provide domain knowledge.
- Scorecards define quality.
- Iteration creates excellence.
- Humans remain responsible for consequential decisions.

## 4. UNIVERSAL PROMPT FAMILY

Use concise, purpose-specific prompts rather than unnecessarily giant prompts.

Understand:
"Understand what I am trying to accomplish, what success looks like, who this is for, and what matters most. Ask me anything you need to know before creating the result."

Questions:
"Ask me every question you need answered to produce the best possible result. Do not ask unnecessary questions."

Known context:
"Based on everything you already know, answer whatever you can yourself. Then ask me only for what you still need."

Create prompt:
"Create the most effective prompt for achieving my goal using everything you now know. Make it clear, precise, complete, and optimized for the best possible result."

Create result:
"Now create the best possible result based on everything we have established. Follow the requirements carefully and use your full capabilities."

Score:
"Score this result against the appropriate quality criteria. Tell me what is working, what is weak, what is missing, and what would make it exceptional."

Why not 10:
"Why is this not a 10? Identify the specific gaps preventing it from being exceptional, while preserving everything that already works."

Improve:
"Improve the result based on your evaluation. Preserve the strengths, fix the weaknesses, and make the smallest changes necessary to create the biggest improvement."

Regression:
"Compare this version with the previous version. Confirm that the improvements are real and that nothing important was lost."

Final quality:
"Perform a final quality check. Verify accuracy, completeness, clarity, usefulness, audience fit, consistency, and overall quality. Identify anything that still needs improvement."

Freeze:
"This is the approved version. Treat it as the source of truth. Do not change anything unless I explicitly ask you to create a new version."

## 5. GOLDEN RULES

1. Do not ask AI to simply make something "good." Define purpose, audience, constraints, success, and quality.
2. Never assume the first answer is the best answer.
3. Never improve blindly; identify what works, what does not, why, and what should change.
4. The goal is better results, not more AI or more prompting.
5. Keep human intention and judgment at the center.
6. AI should do the heavy lifting it can reasonably do.
7. Specialized knowledge + Master Key + role activation + judgment + scoring + iteration create exceptional work.

## 6. ASSESSMENT CONTENT RULE

Each assessment question should teach a small insight without contaminating the participant's answer. The goal is to make the assessment useful even for people who never convert afterward.

## 7. FINAL VALUE PROMISE

15 questions. 15 small opportunities to notice something useful. 1 honest score. 1 clear diagnosis. 1 next best step.

Human Maximus principle:
**Serve humans. Tell the truth. Think deeply. Simplify intelligently. Build beautifully. Optimize relentlessly. Ship AAA. Scale wisdom.**
