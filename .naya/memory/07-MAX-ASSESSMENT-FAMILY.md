# 07 — MAX ASSESSMENT FAMILY

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT MEMORY
Version: 1.0

## The three MAX assessments

### AI MAX
Question: How well do I work with AI?
Purpose: Measure practical AI capability and immediately useful AI mastery.

### MAX LIFE
Question: How well am I operating my life?
Purpose: Reveal patterns in choices, habits, direction, relationships, growth, responsibility, and lived capability.

### MAX PERCEPTION
Question: How deeply and accurately do I perceive life?
Purpose: Reveal how a person interprets reality and whether they can recognize, examine, challenge, and expand their own perception.

## Naming architecture

The family intentionally creates two simultaneous readings:

AI MAX
MAX LIFE
MAX PERCEPTION

Read the categories: AI · LIFE · PERCEPTION.
Read the shared brand: MAX · MAX · MAX.

The combined semantic idea is:

MAX your intelligence.
MAX your life.
MAX your perception.

AI = artificial intelligence.
LIFE = lived experience.
PERCEPTION = the lens through which experience is interpreted.

MAX PERCEPTION is also a conceptual bridge into Human Maximus: AI can improve, life can improve, but a person's experience of both is filtered through perception.

## MAX PERCEPTION design law

MAX PERCEPTION must not become a generic positivity/personality quiz.

Questions should reveal:
- how a person interprets reality
- whether they recognize assumptions
- whether they can see another perspective
- whether they distinguish observation/fact from interpretation
- whether they recognize their own cognitive filters
- whether they are willing to reconsider a belief
- whether they can hold apparently conflicting perspectives
- whether they can change interpretation when new information appears
- whether they understand that perception influences experience
- whether they distinguish what happened from what they made it mean

The assessment should demonstrate perception through the question journey, not merely ask the user whether they are perceptive.

## Proposed five dimensions of perception

1. Awareness — Do I notice what I am actually experiencing?
2. Perspective — Can I see beyond my immediate interpretation?
3. Discernment — Can I distinguish observation from assumption?
4. Flexibility — Can I reconsider when new information appears?
5. Expansion — Can I intentionally choose a broader, deeper interpretation?

These are the initial design dimensions and may evolve through question development and evidence.

## Desired MAX PERCEPTION experience

MAX PERCEPTION → score → perception profile → five-dimensional fingerprint → strongest perception capability → perception blind spot → OH... THAT'S WHY → deeper perception pathway → Human Maximus Codex.

The intended emotional response is recognition and curiosity, not shame. A lower score is an invitation to explore, not a judgment of intelligence or worth.

## Product bridge

The assessment should naturally lead toward the Human Maximus Codex as a deeper perception-development experience. Position the Codex as an expansion pathway, not as a pressure sale.

## Shared assessment engine law

AI MAX, MAX LIFE, and MAX PERCEPTION should share a reusable, configuration-driven assessment engine:

Assessment Configuration → Questions → Responses → Dimensions → Scoring → Result Contract → Reusable Results Engine → Assessment-specific interpretation.

Do not rebuild the engine separately for each assessment.
