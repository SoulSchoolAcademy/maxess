09 · The Three Keys

Exceptional results are a process.

AI can produce something useful immediately. Exceptional work usually comes from directing, evaluating, and improving the first result.

KEY 01

KNOW

Know what you are actually trying to accomplish, who it is for, and what “excellent” needs to mean.

Start with the outcome, not the prompt.
KEY 02

DIRECT

Tell AI what matters. Give it context, constraints, priorities, examples, and a clear definition of success.

Make the target visible.
KEY 03

SCORE

Do not assume the first output is the best output. Score it against weighted criteria, find what is missing, improve it, and repeat.

Create → Score → Improve → Repeat.

Try this the next time AI gives you an answer.

Tell it to score its work against the criteria that matter most. Weight the important criteria more heavily. Then ask what is keeping the result from a 10 and have it improve the work. You are not chasing a number. You are creating a repeatable quality loop.
