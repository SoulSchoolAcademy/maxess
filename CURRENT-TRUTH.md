## Data rule

`window.MAXESS_RESULT` (or the supported equivalent result object) is authoritative. The renderer may interpret it, visualize it, and generate guidance from it, but must not invent a user's score or assessment outcome.

For cross-domain handoffs, do not treat localStorage as a secure cross-origin transport. Use the project's real result contract/bridge when the Naya destination is a different origin. Same-document handoff may use the explicit MAXESS Naya context bridge.
